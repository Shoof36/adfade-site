AdFade Support

Help with AdFade, the TV remote whose Commercial button mutes the TV and plays your music.

Contact

Email kas.holding.llc@gmail.com. Say which TV you have and what happened; in the app, Settings → Help → Send Diagnostics makes a report you can attach.

AdFade can't find my TV

  • Your iPhone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network, and not cellular).
  • The TV has to be on the first time, and may ask you to allow AdFade or show a PIN.
  • On your iPhone: Settings → AdFade → Local Network must be on.
  • Still nothing? On the Find Your TV screen tap Add Manually and enter the TV's address (your TV shows it in its network settings).

Power doesn't turn my TV on

A TV that is off only listens if its own setting allows it: look in the TV's settings for "Turn on via Wi-Fi", "Power On with Mobile" or "Quick Start". Use AdFade once while the TV is on, so it learns how to wake it.

The Commercial button shows a lock

Commercial breaks, and radio between breaks, are part of AdFade Pro; the remote is free. If you already subscribed, open Settings → AdFade Pro → Restore Purchases.

Cancel or change AdFade Pro

In the app: Settings → AdFade Pro → Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple bills the subscription and handles refunds (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Using Spotify, Pandora or another music app

In the Music tab choose Music App and follow Set It Up Once: it adds a shortcut that presses Play in your music app, because iPhone apps aren't allowed to press Play in each other.

Report an ad

The free version shows one banner ad from Google. If an ad is inappropriate, email the address above with what it showed and about when, so it can be reported to Google and blocked.
